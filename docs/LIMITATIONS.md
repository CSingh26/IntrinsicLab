# Where this model can mislead

- A DCF is conditional on assumptions; sensitivity cells are neither forecast probabilities nor confidence bounds.
- Historical profitability does not establish sustainable margin or future ROIC. Growth, reinvestment and financing must be economically compatible.
- Terminal margins and ROIC can jump at the forecast boundary. The analyst must assess a credible transition. Growth below zero assumes indefinite disinvestment in the Gordon formulation.
- Constant tax rates and cash taxes on positive EBIT omit NOL balances, jurisdictional effects and deferred taxes. WACC assumes tax shields can be used.
- One annual currency and end-year convention; no mid-year/stub-period discounting, inflation decomposition or currency conversion.
- Market financing values belong in WACC. Using book values can distort the opportunity cost. Constant leverage and beta may be unrealistic.
- Simplified enterprise bridge requires the analyst to classify operating cash, leases, pensions, options, noncontrolling interests and other claims appropriately. Diluted shares are a point-in-time assumption, not a stock compensation forecast.
- Banks, insurers, resource assets, distress and real options often need materially different valuation methods.
- Imported statements carry user-asserted provenance. The system validates structure and units, not whether a filing citation is authentic or figures are audited.
- Comparable multiples do not control automatically for growth, quality, leverage, cyclicality or accounting differences.
- No real-time provider, SEC parser, AI interpretation, trading, lending decision or personalized recommendation exists in this standalone valuation engine.
